End-to-End Workflow Design

Prepared for Dhanush.
The complete consultant journey from initial setup to a submitted job application.

The 4 Human Gates

Nothing about a consultant is used until a human has explicitly approved it. These gates ensure absolute control.

1. Profile Approval

No unverified personal details reach an employer. A reviewer must check every field.

2. Criteria Activation

No job searching begins until a recruiter confirms the titles, pay, and keywords.

3. Answer Approval

No form is filled with an unapproved answer. Salary & work-auth go strictly to the Owner.

4. Final Submit Review

The machine NEVER submits alone. The consultant must click submit on every single application.
